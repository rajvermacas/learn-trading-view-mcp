### Fundamental Analysis

#### Company Overview
**National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO)** is a Navratna PSU under the Ministry of Mines, Government of India. It is one of the largest integrated aluminium companies in Asia, with operations spanning bauxite mining, alumina refining, aluminium smelting, and power generation.

| Trailing P/E | 13.2x | Cheap relative to profitability |

At 13.2x trailing earnings, NALCO trades at a discount to broader Indian metals sector multiples despite exceptional profitability. The dividend yield of 3.4% provides meaningful income support.

The 42% operating margin is a standout metric — it reflects NALCO's integrated structure (captive bauxite and captive power from its Angul power plant) which insulates it from input cost volatility faced by pure-play smelters. This is a genuine competitive moat.
