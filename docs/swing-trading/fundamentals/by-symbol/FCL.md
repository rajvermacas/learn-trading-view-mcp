## Ranking Packet

**Company:** Fineotex Chemical Ltd
**Analysis Date:** 2026-05-30

**Sponsorship Verdict:** Mixed-to-Strong Sponsorship
**Confidence:** Medium

**Recent Trigger:** Strong. Q4 FY26 consolidated revenue rose 162% YoY to Rs 313.73 Cr, EBITDA rose 105% to Rs 43.69 Cr, and PAT rose 118% to Rs 43.79 Cr. Results were approved on 2026-05-15.

**Balance Sheet Comfort:** Strong with monitoring required. FY26 long-term borrowings were Rs 3.82 Cr and short-term borrowings Rs 4.38 Cr against shareholders' funds of Rs 939.73 Cr. The acquisition raised goodwill and working-capital exposure.
**Catalyst Credibility:** Credible. CrudeChem adds a US oilfield-chemicals platform, approximately 80,000 MTPA capacity, proprietary products, established customers, and larger-contract capability.
**Evidence-to-Price Alignment:** Positive. The >50% three-month move aligns with acquisition integration, a sharply stronger Q4, and capacity expansion.
**Near-Term Fragility:** Medium. Risks include weaker legacy standalone growth, acquisition integration, margin dilution, receivables conversion, oilfield demand variability, raw-material volatility, and post-results digestion.

**Why It Ranks Here:** FCL has a recent, measurable earnings trigger and a credible structural catalyst. Q4 consolidated scale-up materially improves the sponsorship case.
**What Keeps It From Ranking Higher:** Acceleration is substantially inorganic. Core standalone revenue contracted, consolidated margins diluted, and working capital expanded sharply.
**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Above It:** A peer with similarly strong momentum, broad-based organic growth, stable or expanding margins, and cleaner cash conversion.
**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Below It:** A peer whose rally lacks a recent earnings trigger, whose catalyst remains speculative, or whose balance sheet cannot support execution.

## Sponsorship Reasoning

**Peer Placement Notes:** Place FCL above stale or narrative-only momentum names and below peers with organic operating breadth, margin expansion, and stronger cash conversion.
