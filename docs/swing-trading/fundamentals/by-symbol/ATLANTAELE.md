The company says the restriction is limited to Bihar State Power Holding Company tenders.

**Why It Ranks Here:** Audited acceleration, margin expansion, cash conversion, debt repayment, order-book visibility, and current wins align with the price move.
**What Keeps It From Ranking Higher:** Premium valuation, limited listed history, execution intensity, and the SBPDCL debarment prevent an unqualified highest-conviction placement.
**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Above It:** A peer with comparable acceleration and order visibility, but lower valuation stretch, cleaner working capital, and no fresh adverse disclosure.
**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Below It:** A peer with sentiment-led momentum, weaker cash conversion, debt stress, stale catalysts, or materially less visible revenue conversion.
