**Why The Rally Looks Legit Or Fragile For 1-8 Weeks:** The rally followed material disclosures but is fragile because the main catalyst is public, earnings quality is non-recurring, and regulatory and dilution overhangs can amplify volatility.
**Contradictions, Weaknesses, And Open Risks:** Confirm PRV-sale closing, receipt of proceeds, debt reduction, pipeline spending plans, PDP-716 remediation timeline, and fully diluted warrant impact.
**Peer Placement Notes:** Place SPARC in the middle tier above unsponsored momentum names and below businesses showing recurring operating acceleration with cleaner earnings quality.
