**Company:** Indiabulls Limited
**Analysis Date:** 2026-05-13
**Screen Thesis Fit:** Fits strongly on visible momentum and recent operating change: 3-month return 101.13%, market cap Rs 3,789.97 Cr, Q4/FY26 turnaround after merger.
**Sponsorship Verdict:** Mixed to Strongly Sponsored
**Confidence:** Medium

**Recent Trigger:** Strong. The key trigger is the 2026-04-29 Q4/FY26 result and investor update: consolidated Q4 revenue rose to Rs 408.53 Cr and owner-attributable PAT was Rs 196.00 Cr versus a year-ago loss, while FY26 owner-attributable PAT was Rs 273.87 Cr versus a loss in FY25.

**Operating Evidence:** Strong but newly formed. Management disclosed FY26 real-estate sales booked of Rs 2,752 Cr, collections of Rs 400 Cr, 909 units sold, and 21.6 lakh sq ft area sold; only Rs 259 Cr revenue was recognized from sales booked, leaving Rs 2,493 Cr to be recognized.

**Earnings Quality:** Mixed. Q4 profit is not just narrative: revenue and operating profit improved sharply. However, Q4 PAT benefited materially from deferred tax credit, and the merged entity has limited clean comparable history. Treat the reported profit as real evidence, but not yet a fully proven run-rate.

**Balance Sheet Comfort:** Acceptable, not clean. FY26 paid-up equity plus other equity was about Rs 3,083 Cr, and the ARC platform disclosed 97.92% capital adequacy with Rs 533 Cr net worth. Comfort is helped by the equity base and disclosed ARC capitalization, but reduced by business complexity across real estate, broking, ARC, SMB finance, and post-merger accounting.

**Catalyst Credibility:** Strong. The October 2025 merger created a simplified Indiabulls Limited structure; Q4 then showed real-estate-led profit recognition and management disclosed Rs 21,366 Cr GDV across 110.52 lakh sq ft, including Rs 6,029 Cr planned FY27 launches.

**Evidence-to-Price Alignment:** Plausible but stretched. A 101% three-month move is large, but it aligns with a visible earnings inflection, FII holding rising to 17.00% by March 2026, and a cleaner post-merger story. The rally is not purely disconnected, but much of the obvious Q4 catalyst may already be priced.

**Near-Term Fragility:** Medium-high. Fragility comes from sharp price appreciation, dependence on real-estate execution and revenue recognition timing, deferred-tax-aided PAT optics, and merger comparability. Any delay in FY27 launches, weaker collections, or Q1 FY27 disappointment can hit sentiment quickly.

**Why It Ranks Here:** IBULLSLTD deserves an upper-pack fundamental sponsorship rank because the price move has a clear trigger: post-merger simplification, Q4 profit swing, real-estate bookings, disclosed revenue visibility, and improved institutional ownership.

**What Keeps It From Ranking Higher:** The sponsorship is not yet clean enough for top-tier treatment because the operating history is short, earnings quality is affected by tax/accounting items, and the rally has already run hard.

**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Above It:** A peer with comparable momentum plus cleaner recurring earnings, stronger cash conversion, less accounting complexity, and multiple quarters of operating confirmation.

**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Below It:** A peer where price momentum is driven mainly by valuation rerating, vague announcements, or one-off profit without visible sales, collections, or near-term execution pipeline.

## 2. Sponsorship Reasoning

**Why Sponsorship Is Present Or Not:** Sponsorship is present, but not pristine. The move is backed by a real change in reported fundamentals: Q4 FY26 revenue, PAT, real-estate sales bookings, and post-merger business structure all improved materially. The market is likely rewarding the shift from a messy legacy/holding-company profile toward a real-estate-led operating story with visible launch and recognition pipeline.

**Recent Trigger And What Likely Drove The Move:** The most credible recent trigger is the 2026-04-29 Q4/FY26 result plus investor presentation. Q4 consolidated operating revenue rose sharply, Q4 owner-attributable net profit moved to Rs 196 Cr from a year-ago loss, and management framed FY26 as a transformational post-merger year. This fits the screen's momentum thesis: a large price move with identifiable operating and structural triggers.

**Business And Financial Evidence:** The strongest evidence is in real estate. FY26 sales booked of Rs 2,752 Cr, collections of Rs 400 Cr, and Rs 2,493 Cr balance revenue to be recognized from booked sales provide a tangible bridge from bookings to future accounting revenue. The company also disclosed Rs 21,366 Cr GDV across launched, FY27, and future projects. Broking remains relevant but not the main growth driver; ARC adds optionality with Rs 3,794 Cr asset under collection and Rs 288 Cr FY26 recoveries.

**Catalyst Context:** The catalyst is credible because it is not only a headline result. The NCLT-approved scheme became effective on 2025-10-14, and the merged entity now combines real estate, financial services, broking, ARC, equipment leasing, and SMB platform exposure. The market appears to be underwriting a cleaner Indiabulls platform with real estate as the anchor.

**Why The Rally Looks Legit Or Fragile For 1-8 Weeks:** For a 1-8 week swing, the rally looks legitimate enough to remain in consideration because fresh Q4 evidence, institutional ownership improvement, and launch pipeline can keep the story active. It is fragile because the stock has already doubled in three months, Q4 profit includes a large deferred tax benefit, and real-estate revenue recognition is execution-timed. The sponsorship is good, but the margin for disappointment is thin.

**Contradictions, Weaknesses, And Open Risks:** The biggest contradiction is between strong headline PAT and mixed earnings quality. Q4 profit improved, but tax credits and post-merger comparability reduce confidence in the normalized run-rate. The business is also complex for a swing dossier: real estate, broking, ARC, and SMB finance each carry different risk cycles. Near-term event risk is mainly any adverse clarification, weak Q1 FY27 update, launch delay, or profit-recognition disappointment.

**Peer Placement Notes:** IBULLSLTD should rank above momentum names with no operating trigger or no identifiable catalyst. It should rank below names where recent profit growth is cleaner, cash-backed, repeated across quarters, and less dependent on merger/accounting normalization. Overall placement: upper-middle to high sponsorship bucket, but not the cleanest top-rank candidate.
