**Near-Term Fragility:** Medium-high. The audited-results risk has passed, but valuation assumes continued rapid execution. Export tender timing, margin sustainability, working-capital discipline, capacity ramp-up, and non-core investment decisions can drive volatility.

**Why It Ranks Here:** CUPID merits a high sponsorship rank because the >50% three-month momentum has unusually strong operating confirmation: record Q4 results, FY26 guidance beat, improved cash generation, better working-capital metrics, and a visible FY27 growth roadmap.
**What Keeps It From Ranking Higher:** Trailing valuation is stretched, non-operating income remains meaningful, FY27 targets require another major step-up, and capital allocation into Baazar Style Retail adds risk outside the core operating thesis.
**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Above It:** A company with comparable audited growth and catalysts but lower valuation, cleaner operating-profit conversion, less aggressive forward assumptions, and no material non-core investment risk.
**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Below It:** A company whose rally lacks audited revenue and EBITDA confirmation, relies mainly on narrative catalysts, or has weaker balance-sheet and cash-flow evidence.
