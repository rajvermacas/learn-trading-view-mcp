## 2. Sponsorship Reasoning

**Why Sponsorship Is Present Or Not:** Sponsorship is present. Engineers India's 33.00% three-month return is backed by recent operating evidence rather than only price momentum. The Q3 FY26 print showed sharp YoY revenue and profit growth, all-time-high order-book narrative, and strong balance-sheet comfort.

**Recent Trigger And What Likely Drove The Move:** The main trigger was the February 2026 Q3 FY26 result and follow-on order-book commentary. The market likely reacted to the scale of earnings surprise, record order-book messaging, and visibility commentary.

**Business And Financial Evidence:** Standalone Q3 FY26 total income was Rs 12,515.91 million, PAT was Rs 3,017.36 million, and EPS was Rs 5.37. For 9M FY26, total income was Rs 30,919.42 million and PAT was Rs 4,867.24 million. Consultancy remained a high-margin anchor while turnkey delivered the quarterly profit uplift.

**Catalyst Context:** The catalyst is credible because it is not only a one-quarter P&L event. The order book was Rs 12,537.9 crore as of December 2025, split around 60% consultancy and 40% turnkey, and later indicated near Rs 15,670 crore after new assignments.

**Why The Rally Looks Legit Or Fragile For 1-8 Weeks:** The rally is mostly legitimate because price has earnings, order-book, and balance-sheet sponsorship. Fragility comes from one-off settlement help and immediate FY26 result risk.

**Contradictions, Weaknesses, And Open Risks:** The main contradiction is strong headline profit growth versus partly one-off earnings quality. Other risks are execution delays, slower capex awards, skilled manpower constraints, and project settlement timing.

**Peer Placement Notes:** Rank above momentum names with no earnings acceleration or order-book visibility; below peers with cleaner recurring growth and fewer one-off adjustments.
