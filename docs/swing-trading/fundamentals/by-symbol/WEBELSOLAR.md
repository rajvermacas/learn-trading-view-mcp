## 1. Ranking Packet

**Symbol:** WEBELSOLAR
**Company:** Websol Energy System Ltd.
**Analysis Date:** 2026-05-13
**Screen Thesis Fit:** Strong fit. The stock is in the high-momentum, high-market-cap universe with 3-month return of 54.08%, market cap of Rs 4,693.10 Cr, Q4FY26 sales growth of 132.07%, PAT growth of about 158%, ROCE above 60%, and no visible pre-rank penalty flags.
**Sponsorship Verdict:** Strongly Sponsored, with monitorable fragility
**Confidence:** High

**Recent Trigger:** Strong positive. The main trigger is the Q4FY26/FY26 result release on 2026-04-27/28: revenue from operations rose to Rs 401 Cr in Q4FY26, PAT to about Rs 125 Cr, and FY26 PAT to Rs 303 Cr, supported by high utilization and order-book visibility.

**Operating Evidence:** Strong. The rally is backed by real operating scale-up: cell capacity doubled to 1.2 GW, Q4 cell utilization remained above 90%, module utilization was 74%, and the order book was Rs 1,161 Cr as of 2026-03-31. Management said the book can be executed within one financial year in the earnings call.

**Earnings Quality:** Good, not perfect. FY26 operating cash flow was Rs 255 Cr, roughly 84% of PAT, which supports profit quality. However, Q4 EBITDA margin fell to 36.4% from 45.4% YoY and 40.8% QoQ, with mix shift toward modules, silver cost pressure, and softening realizations. FY27 tax rate may normalize after utilization of prior tax credits.

**Balance Sheet Comfort:** Good near term, execution-sensitive medium term. Websol ended FY26 with total debt of Rs 118 Cr, cash of Rs 152 Cr, net cash of Rs 34 Cr, net worth of Rs 631 Cr, debt/equity of 0.19x, and CRISIL BBB+/Stable per management. Comfort is reduced by the next capex cycle: Rs 250-270 Cr TOPCon upgrade plus a larger Rs 2,800-3,000 Cr expansion plan.

**Catalyst Credibility:** Strong. Catalysts are not only narrative: Q4 utilization, order book, DCR demand, ALMM tailwinds, TOPCon upgrade to lift cell capacity to 1.35 GW, and the planned integrated capacity expansion are all tied to operating plans. The near-term catalyst stack is credible, though the Andhra Pradesh expansion still needs clearer funding and execution milestones.

**Evidence-to-Price Alignment:** Strong. A 54% 3-month move is large, but it is aligned with a record quarter, 82.4% FY26 revenue growth, 95.8% FY26 PAT growth, net-cash turn, and a P/E around 15.4 from the screen data. The price move does not look purely speculative; it is responding to visible earnings acceleration.

**Near-Term Fragility:** Medium. Key fragilities are promoter pledge overhang, possible margin compression from module mix and silver/realization movements, execution risk on TOPCon and greenfield expansion, and a potential sentiment hit if the promised pledge release or expansion clarity slips. Management said more than 80% of promoter shares were pledged against the IREDA facility and targeted repayment/release within one to two months in the earnings call.

**Why It Ranks Here:** Websol deserves a high sponsorship rank because momentum is supported by recent audited earnings, capacity utilization, cash conversion, order-book visibility, and identifiable policy/technology catalysts.

**What Keeps It From Ranking Higher:** Margin compression, very high promoter-share pledge context, dependence on DCR/ALMM-linked demand, and large upcoming capex prevent a cleaner top-of-list classification.

**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Above It:** A peer with similar or better earnings acceleration, high utilization, and order visibility, but lower promoter pledge risk, clearer capex funding, and more stable margins.

**What Kind Of Peer Should Rank Below It:** A peer whose 3-month rally is based mainly on sector narrative, weak or delayed earnings support, poorer cash conversion, or a less visible order/capacity catalyst.

## 2. Sponsorship Reasoning

**Why Sponsorship Is Present Or Not:** Sponsorship is present. The move is backed by concrete business evidence: Q4FY26 revenue rose 132.1% YoY to Rs 401 Cr, PAT rose about 158% YoY to Rs 125 Cr, FY26 PAT nearly doubled to Rs 303 Cr, and FY26 operating cash flow was Rs 255 Cr. The company also turned net cash surplus and entered FY27 with high utilization plus a Rs 1,161 Cr order book. This is the type of operating proof that can justify momentum participation.

**Recent Trigger And What Likely Drove The Move:** The immediate driver was the late-April Q4FY26 result and call. The market received a package of record quarterly earnings, strong FY26 cash generation, high cell utilization, a confirmed order book, and TOPCon upgrade commentary. Additional support came from the prior February 2026 module orders and the broader DCR/ALMM policy setup, but the April results are the cleanest trigger.

**Business And Financial Evidence:** Websol is now running a materially larger platform: current solar cell capacity is 1.2 GW and module capacity is 550 MW. Cell utilization stayed above 90% in Q4, and module utilization reached 74%. FY26 revenue was Rs 1,049 Cr versus Rs 575 Cr in FY25; EBITDA was Rs 429 Cr; PAT was Rs 303 Cr. Balance-sheet evidence improved sharply with net worth rising to about Rs 631 Cr, debt/equity falling to 0.19x, and net cash of Rs 34 Cr. Cash conversion is a major positive because it reduces the risk that the earnings are only accounting-led.

**Catalyst Context:** The catalyst stack is credible: ALMM/DCR-linked domestic demand, PM-KUSUM and PM Surya Ghar exposure through customers, confirmed orders, TOPCon upgrade, and future integrated expansion. Management indicated the TOPCon upgrade should start commercial production around February 2027 and raise cell capacity to 1.35 GW. The larger expansion is still more execution-dependent, but the company says the June 2027 timeline remains in place.

**Why The Rally Looks Legit Or Fragile For 1-8 Weeks:** For a 1-8 week swing, the rally looks fundamentally legitimate because the evidence is fresh, audited, and price-relevant. The valuation in the screen, around 15.4x P/E, is not obviously detached from the earnings acceleration. The fragile part is that the stock has already moved 54% in three months, and any delay in pledge release, order conversion, ALMM-linked demand, or expansion clarity could trigger a fast de-rating. Margin compression is also a live issue: EBITDA margin fell materially in Q4 despite strong absolute EBITDA growth.

**Contradictions, Weaknesses, And Open Risks:** The strongest contradiction is that operating and balance-sheet metrics improved, yet promoter pledge risk remains significant. Management's stated plan to repay the IREDA facility and release pledged shares within one to two months is now a near-term checkpoint. Working capital also rose with scale-up; management says receivables are partly LC-backed, but this still needs monitoring. Other risks are lower module margins versus cells, silver cost pressure, realization softening, normal tax rate from FY27, TOPCon execution risk, and debt needs for the large expansion.

**Peer Placement Notes:** Within the screen universe, Websol should rank near the top of the fundamental sponsorship set because it combines momentum, fresh earnings acceleration, utilization proof, order-book visibility, and balance-sheet improvement. It should not automatically outrank a peer with similarly strong earnings but cleaner governance/pledge risk and lower capex uncertainty. It should outrank names where the 3-month move is not backed by comparable sales/PAT growth, cash flow, or identifiable operating catalysts.
