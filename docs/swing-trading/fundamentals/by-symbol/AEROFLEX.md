# Sponsorship Reasoning

- **Why Sponsorship Is Present Or Not**: Sponsorship is present. The stock’s price strength is now anchored in audited FY26 delivery, not just a theme. The new liquid-cooling business is no longer hypothetical: 617 skid assemblies were sold in FY26 and the company disclosed roughly Rs 21.2 Cr of related revenue. Source: [transcript](https://www.aeroflexindia.com/wp-content/uploads/Transcript-for-Q4-Earning-Conference-call-held-on-May-06-2026.pdf).
- **Recent Trigger And What Likely Drove The Move**: The proximate trigger is the May 5 board approval and May 6 results call, which confirmed the highest quarterly and yearly performance to date. The April 1 capacity step-up to 6,000 skids/year likely helped the market believe the demand is real, and the May 20 Goldman Sachs group meeting keeps institutional attention on the name. Sources: [board outcome](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/AEROFLEXINDIA_05052026192956_outcomeofbm.pdf), [capacity update](https://www.aeroflexindia.com/wp-content/uploads/Intimation-of-update-in-expansion-of-Capacity-01.04.2026.pdf), [investor meet](https://www.aeroflexindia.com/wp-content/uploads/Intimation-of-Analyst-Institutional-Investor-Meet-20.05.2026.pdf).
- **Business And Financial Evidence**: FY26 total income was Rs 443.3 Cr and PAT Rs 55.5 Cr. Q4 total income was Rs 126.5 Cr, EBITDA Rs 30 Cr, and PAT Rs 17.6 Cr. EBITDA margin was 22.6% for FY26 and 23.86% in Q4. Value-added products were 52% of FY26 sales, and domestic sales increased to 31% from 26% as the new segment started contributing more meaningfully. Source: [transcript](https://www.aeroflexindia.com/wp-content/uploads/Transcript-for-Q4-Earning-Conference-call-held-on-May-06-2026.pdf).
- **Catalyst Context**: The thesis is supported by a long-term exclusive supply arrangement, a visible quarterly pipeline, and a staged capacity expansion from 2,000 to 6,000 units/year with a target of 15,000 by June/July 2026. That gives the move a concrete operating basis instead of a vague data-center theme. Source: [transcript](https://www.aeroflexindia.com/wp-content/uploads/Transcript-for-Q4-Earning-Conference-call-held-on-May-06-2026.pdf), [capacity update](https://www.aeroflexindia.com/wp-content/uploads/Intimation-of-update-in-expansion-of-Capacity-01.04.2026.pdf). The open GST order is a separate risk, but the company has said it intends to appeal and does not expect a material financial impact. Source: [GST notice](https://www.aeroflexindia.com/wp-content/uploads/Intimation-under-Regulation-30-dated-08.04.2026.pdf).
- **Why The Rally Looks Legit Or Fragile For 1-8 Weeks**: Legit because the fundamentals finally caught up enough to explain the rerating: record results, a real product ramp, and a clear second-stage catalyst calendar. Fragile because the rerating is already large, the stock is expensive on current earnings, the new segment is still small relative to the full business, and near-term execution details can still fall short of what the chart is pricing.
- **Contradictions, Weaknesses, And Open Risks**: The key tension is valuation versus proof. Price has moved faster than annual earnings growth, the commercial story is still concentrated around a small number of relationships, and the GST demand is a genuine open item. The May 20 meet and the June/July capacity target are checkpoints; if either disappoints, the market can de-rate the name quickly.
- **Peer Placement Notes**: This sits ahead of momentum names that lack audited conversion, but behind peers that combine similar growth with lower multiples and cleaner cash generation. The sponsorship is real, but the market is already paying for some future delivery.
